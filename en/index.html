Questions

Is Refeel free?

Yes. You can log your days and see your calendar and essential statistics for free, with no time limit. Premium, a monthly or yearly subscription, unlocks all discoveries, in-depth analyses, perspectives and the PDF journal.

Do I need an account?

No. Refeel opens straight away, with no sign-up and no email address.

Can anyone read my journal?

No. Your journal, photos and discoveries stay on your iPhone. Nobody reads them, not us, not anyone else.

Does Refeel use artificial intelligence?

No. Your discoveries are calculations made on your iPhone, from your own days. Nothing you write is sent to an AI.

How do I keep everything when I change iPhone?

In Settings, Backup & restore, create a backup and save the file to iCloud Drive. On your new iPhone, tap “Restore a backup”.

Is Refeel available on Android?

Soon! Refeel is available on iPhone today, and the Android version is coming to Google Play.

Does Refeel replace medical care?

No. Refeel is a wellbeing journal, not a medical device. If you are going through a hard time, talk to a health professional.
