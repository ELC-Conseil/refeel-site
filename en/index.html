Note your day.
Discover what does you good.

Refeel is a wellbeing journal for iPhone. Each day, you note your mood in a few seconds, then, if you like, your emotions, what you did, your sleep or a photo. Little by little, Refeel shows you what comes back in your good and not-so-good days.

Coming soon to the App Store

Simply note

One mood out of five, then whatever you want to add. No judgment, no diagnosis.

See what comes back

A calendar, clear statistics and discoveries drawn from your own days.

Stay private

Your journal stays on your iPhone. Nobody reads your notes, and you can export or erase everything.
