Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Refeel is designed so that your journal stays yours. This page explains, simply, what data exists, where it goes and what you can do with it.

What stays on your iPhone

Your journal (moods, emotions, activities, events, energy, sleep, stress, notes), your photos, your discoveries and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. Statistics and discoveries are computed on your phone. We have no access to your journal and nobody reads your notes.

What is sent

Two anonymous answers, only once

At the end of the app introduction, your answers to two questions (what you would like to find in your journal, how you discovered Refeel) are sent once, along with the app language and version. No name, no identifier, nothing from your journal. They are stored by our host Supabase in the European Union (Ireland) and are used only to improve Refeel.

Feedback on perspectives, only if you agree

If you turn on the option in Settings, Privacy, your “Helpful” or “Not for me” feedback on perspectives is sent anonymously (the card, the feedback and the day), without your journal or any identifier. You can turn it off at any time.

Premium subscription

Purchases are made and billed by Apple. We never see your name, email address or payment details. To know whether Premium is active, the app uses the RevenueCat service, which receives an anonymous identifier created by the app and the purchase information provided by Apple (product, date, status). RevenueCat receives nothing from your journal.

Messages to support

When you write to us, we receive your message and your email address. A technical diagnostic (version, device, errors) is attached only if you choose so, and never contains your notes, photos or moods.

Backup, notifications, Face ID and photos

  • Backup and export: they create a file you keep wherever you like (for example in Files or iCloud Drive). We have no access to it.
  • Notifications: your reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. No server sends them.
  • Face ID: the check is done by iOS. The app only receives “succeeded” or “failed”, never your face.
  • Photos: the app only accesses the photos you choose to add, and keeps them on your iPhone.

What we don’t do

No account to create, no ads, no ad tracking, no selling of data, no audience measurement in the app.

Your rights

You can export all your data or erase everything from the app, in Settings, My data. Since the data sent is anonymous, we cannot link it to you. For any question about your data, write to us at support@elc-conseil.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Controller

ELC Conseil, publisher of Refeel. Contact: support@elc-conseil.com.

Refeel is not intended for children under 13. If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
