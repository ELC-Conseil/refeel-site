Frequently asked questions about Refeel

Here are the questions we’re asked most often. If yours isn’t here, write to us at support@elc-conseil.com.

What is Refeel?

A private journal for iPhone. Each day, you log your mood in a few seconds, then, if you like, your emotions, what you did, your sleep or a photo. Little by little, Refeel shows you what comes back in your good and not-so-good days.

Who is Refeel for?

Anyone who wants to better understand what does them good, without spending hours on it. No need to love writing: one mood a day is enough to start.

How much time does it take each day?

A few seconds for your mood. A minute if you add your emotions, activities or a note. A daily reminder helps you make it a habit.

Is Refeel free?

Yes. You can log your days and see your calendar and essential statistics for free, with no time limit. Premium, a monthly or yearly subscription, unlocks all discoveries, in-depth analyses, perspectives and the PDF journal.

What happens if I stop Premium?

You keep all your data and keep logging your days. Discoveries you already opened stay readable in full, and the Face ID lock stays on.

Do I need an account?

No. Refeel opens straight away, with no sign-up and no email address.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere: your journal, photos and discoveries stay on your iPhone. Nobody reads them, not us, not anyone else. The details are in the privacy policy.

Does Refeel use artificial intelligence?

No. Your discoveries are calculations made on your iPhone, from your own days. Nothing you write is sent to an AI.

How do I keep everything when I change iPhone?

In Settings, Backup & restore, create a backup and save the file to iCloud Drive. On your new iPhone, tap “Restore a backup”.

Is Refeel available on Android?

Soon! Refeel is available on iPhone today, and the Android version is coming to Google Play.

Does Refeel replace medical care?

No. Refeel is a wellbeing journal, not a medical device. If you are going through a hard time, talk to a health professional.
